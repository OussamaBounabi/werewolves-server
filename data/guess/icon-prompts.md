# Legend info icons: 2 sheets

Save them as `icons-1.png` and `icons-2.png`. The bright green background is keyed out into transparency when cut.
Each tile's id (in brackets) is the `icon` value in legends.json.

## icons-1.png — the big awards and cups

```
One square image, 1254 x 1254 px: a 3 x 3 grid of nine equal square tiles with thin gaps between them, every tile and gap on a flat pure bright green background (#00FF00), no shadows on the background, no gradient.
Each tile holds one glossy semi-realistic 3D football award icon, centered, seen from the front, filling about 75% of the tile, warm studio light with soft golden highlights. Original generic designs, not copies of any real trophy.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no flags, no people's faces.
The nine icons, left to right then top to bottom:
1. [ballon] a shiny golden football ball award resting on a small round black base
2. [best] a small golden statuette of a footballer figure striking a ball, on a black base
3. [boot] a golden football boot award on a small black base
4. [glove] a golden goalkeeper glove award, open palm, on a small black base
5. [worldcup] a tall golden trophy topped by a golden globe, held up by abstract swirling golden shapes, on a dark base
6. [ucl] a large polished silver cup with two very big rounded handles
7. [continental] a slim golden cup with two small handles and a small golden star on its lid
8. [clubworld] a golden trophy shaped like a curved shield with a small globe on top
9. [olympic] a gold medal on a red ribbon
```
