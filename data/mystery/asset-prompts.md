# Mystery Item (الشيء الغامض): every picture

Save everything in your "Play Room game/mystery item" folder with the names below; I cut and key them.

## 1. The logo — `logo.png`

```
A game logo on a flat pure bright green background (#00FF00), wide format (about 16:10), centred, nothing touching the edges, no shadow on the background.
The words "MYSTERY ITEM" in big, chunky, rounded 3D bubble letters, glossy like candy: "MYSTERY" on the first line in bright white with a deep purple outline, "ITEM" on the second line in glossy orange-to-yellow gradient with a deep purple outline. A small closed cardboard mystery box with a big glowing purple question mark on its side sits on top of the letters, slightly tilted, with little yellow light rays around it.
Playful mobile-game style, same family as a "GUESS WHO?" logo made of glossy bubble letters. Only those two words, spelled exactly.
```

## 2. The home screen tile — `tile.png`

```
A square game cover, 1024 x 1024 px, semi-realistic 3D cartoon style (Pixar-like), in a dark neon lounge glowing purple and orange.
Four teenage friends (two boys, two girls, different looks) lean around a table in the middle; on it, a big glowing cardboard mystery box is opening, and out of it float silhouettes of things: a lion, a car, an apple, a lamp, a hot air balloon. Each friend holds a small card with a glowing question mark and looks curious, excited or thinking.
At the bottom, the logo "MYSTERY ITEM" in big glossy bubble letters (white "MYSTERY", orange "ITEM", purple outlines) with a small mystery box. Only those two words.
No other text, no brand names.
```

## 3. The background — `background.png`

```
A tall portrait background, 9:21 (for example 720 x 1680 px), no people, no text.
A dark neon game-show stage seen from the audience: deep purple and night blue, warm orange spotlights from the top crossing over the stage, a few big glowing question marks floating in the air, light haze. In the middle of the stage, on a round glowing pedestal, a closed mystery box under a silk cloth.
Keep the top quarter and the bottom half calm and dark (buttons and cards go there); the brightest part around the middle.
```

## 4. The two hidden cards — `secret_blue.png`, `secret_red.png`

```
A portrait card picture, 360 x 393 px proportions, on a pure black background, no text.
A dark navy-blue silhouette of a closed cardboard box, front view, standing in the middle and filling most of the picture, its edges softly lit in electric blue; on its front, a big glowing electric-blue question mark.
Minimal, dark and moody, the same style as a dark footballer silhouette with a glowing question mark on his face.
```
Then the same prompt again with **red** instead of blue (dark wine-red silhouette, glowing red edges and question mark).
